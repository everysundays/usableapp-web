challenge: "Designing a non-monetary economy requires solving complex trust and liquidity problems. How do you prevent inflation of 'time credits'? How do you ensure safety when strangers enter homes? How do you balance supply and demand in a hyper-local (5km) radius without a central market maker?"
solution: "I architected a closed-loop economic model based on 'Time Credits' (1h = 60 credits). The core logic involves: 1) A progressive radius matching algorithm (expands search ring every 2 hours if unmatched). 2) A 'Family Pool' entity allowing cross-generation credit transfer (young earn, elderly spend). 3) A Trust Graph protocol requiring identity verification before 'Home Access' category requests are unlocked."
results:
  - "Economic Model: Self-regulating liquidity via cap-and-floor credit limits"
  - "Safety Architecture: Tiered access control based on user verification level"
  - "Scalability: Geo-sharded database design for hyper-local query performance"

A time-banking platform that rebuilds community connections by enabling neighbors to exchange help using time as currency, creating sustainable mutual support networks in urban Thailand.
